The Essence of EstiMania

EstiMania is a thrilling trick-taking card game of strategy, foresight, and risk management. Unlike traditional card games where having the biggest hand guarantees victory, in EstiMania your success depends entirely on how accurately you can forecast the number of tricks you will win in each round.

Hit your exact estimate, and your score doubles! Miss your estimate by even a single trick, and you lose points for the discrepancy. Every card you play must be calculated to reach your target number of wins — no more, no less.

🎯

Precision Estimation

Bid the exact number of tricks you expect to win each round. Exact bids earn 2x points; zero bids grant a bonus point.

👑

Suit Hierarchy

Suits are strictly ranked: Diamonds beat Spades, Spades beat Hearts, Hearts beat Clubs. Aces are always the highest value.

👀

Indian Poker Finale

In the decisive 1-card final round, you cannot see your own card! You only see your opponents' cards, forcing deductive estimation.

Suit Hierarchy & Card Values

Suit dominance determines the winner whenever cards of different suits are played.

#1
♦

♦ Diamonds (Gold)

Rank 1 • Supreme Suit

Diamonds trump all other suits unconditionally. A 2 of Diamonds will defeat an Ace of Spades!

#2
♠

♠ Spades (Sword)

Rank 2 • High Suit

Spades beat Hearts and Clubs. They are overcome only by Diamonds.

#3
♥

♥ Hearts (Cups)

Rank 3 • Medium Suit

Hearts beat Clubs. They are overcome by Diamonds and Spades.

#4
♣

♣ Clubs (Clubs)

Rank 4 • Base Suit

The foundational suit. Overcome by any card of Diamonds, Spades, or Hearts.

Card Rank Within the Same Suit

When cards of the same suit compete, the standard numerical ranking applies:

A (Ace - Highest) > K > Q > J > 10 > 9 > 8 > 7 > 6 > 5 > 4 > 3 > 2 (Lowest)

The Ace is the undisputed highest card in its suit, outranking the King, Queen, Jack, 10, down to 2.

How to Play: Step-by-Step

1

1. Dealing & Bidding

Each round begins with cards dealt to every player. After inspecting your hand, each player places a bet (from 0 up to the number of cards in hand) on how many tricks they anticipate winning.

2

2. Must Follow Suit

The player leading the trick may play any card. All other players MUST play a card of the same suit as the lead card if they have one. If a player holds no cards of that suit, they may play any card from any other suit.

3

3. Winning the Trick

The highest-ranking card (evaluating suit hierarchy first, then card value) wins the trick. The trick winner gathers the cards and leads the next trick.

4

4. Match Progression (1 ➔ N ➔ 1)

Round 1 starts with 1 card per player. Each successive round adds +1 card up to the match maximum (e.g. 5 or 10 cards), then scales back down round by round to 1 card.

Scoring System

Points are calculated at the end of each round based on the accuracy of your estimate:

🎯 +2 × Bet Points

Exact Bet (Bet > 0)

Winning exactly what you estimated awards double points! E.g., estimating 3 and winning 3 earns +6 points.

Bet: 3 | Won: 3 ➔ +6 Pts
🛡️ +1 Bonus Point

Zero Bet Success (Bet = 0)

Successfully winning 0 tricks when bidding 0 awards 1 point, protecting you during weak hands.

Bet: 0 | Won: 0 ➔ +1 Pt
❌ -|Bet - Won| Points

Missed Estimate

Over-bidding or under-bidding penalizes you by the absolute difference. E.g., betting 3 but winning 1 loses 2 points; betting 0 but winning 2 loses 2 points.

Bet: 3 | Won: 1 ➔ -2 Pts

The Blind Final Round (Indian Poker)

In the final round, each player is dealt a single card, but you CANNOT view your own card! Instead, you hold it up facing outwards — you can see every other player's card, and they can see yours! Based on the cards you see on the table and your memory of previously played cards, you must deduce whether your unseen card is the highest and place your final bet.

Tactics & Master Strategies

🧠

Track Diamonds & Aces

Diamonds are invincible against other suits. If the Ace of Diamonds has already been played, the remaining Diamonds become the absolute masters of the board.

✂️

Control Your Wins

Winning too many tricks is just as damaging as winning too few! Once your bet is satisfied, deliberately discard low or off-suit cards to let others take the lead.

🛡️

The Power of Zero Bids

When dealt middle-tier or low cards with no Diamonds, bidding zero is often the safest path to positive points (+1 pt) while opponents battle and lose points.

🎲

Position Advantage

Being the last to bet gives you complete knowledge of the total table bids, revealing whether the round is over-bid (aggressive) or under-bid (safe).
